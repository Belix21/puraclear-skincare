What does PuraClear do?

PuraClear is an acne-treatment moisturizer with 0.5% salicylic acid. Its labeled uses are treating acne, helping clear most acne blemishes, and helping prevent new acne pimples, blackheads, and whiteheads.

Is it a moisturizer or an acne treatment?

Both. Salicylic acid is the acne-treatment active. The cream also contains moisturizing ingredients including glycerin and dimethicone. It is an acne medication, so follow its Drug Facts directions even though it is a moisturizer.

How do I start using it?

Clean skin thoroughly, then apply a thin layer to the affected area. Start once daily and follow the label. Increase gradually only if needed or as directed by a doctor. If dryness or peeling occurs, reduce frequency.

Can I use other acne treatments with it?

Using another topical acne medication at the same time or immediately after PuraClear makes irritation and dryness more likely. If that happens, use only one topical acne medication at a time. Ask your clinician how it fits with prescribed treatments.

What if my skin feels dry?

Reduce use to once daily or every other day if bothersome dryness or peeling occurs. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation becomes severe. Moisturizing ingredients do not mean an acne treatment cannot cause dryness.

Know your skincare.

Directions & Drug Facts

Active ingredient / Purpose

Salicylic Acid 0.5% / Acne Treatment

Uses

For the treatment of acne. Penetrates pores to clear most acne blemishes. Helps keep skin clear of new acne pimples, blackheads, or whiteheads.

Warnings

For external use only.

Skin irritation and dryness are more likely if you use another topical acne medication at the same time or immediately following use of this product. If this occurs, use only one topical acne medication at a time.

Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation becomes severe.

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use.

Directions

Clean the skin thoroughly before applying this product. Cover the entire affected area with a thin layer one to three times daily. Because excessive drying of the skin may occur, start with one application daily, then gradually increase to two or three times daily if needed or as directed by a doctor. If bothersome dryness or peeling occurs, reduce application to once a day or every other day.

Other information

Protect the product in this container from excessive heat and direct sun.
