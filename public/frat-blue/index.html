Isn’t skincare kind of soft?

You brush your teeth. You wash your hair. Your face deserves the same effort. Caring how you show up is a good thing. Own it.

Wait. Is this actually acne treatment?

Yes. PuraClear Acne Moisturizer contains 0.5% salicylic acid. Its labeled uses are treating acne, clearing most acne blemishes, and helping prevent new pimples, blackheads, and whiteheads. The varsity attitude is extra.

I already wash my face. Why moisturize?

Cleaning and moisturizing do different jobs. This formula combines an acne-treatment active with moisturizing ingredients including glycerin and dimethicone. Keep cleansing; use PuraClear according to the Drug Facts directions.

Will this get me laid?

It treats acne. You still have to be interesting. Try asking her a question.

Can I pile it on before a night out?

Follow the label, not your calendar. Start with a thin layer once daily. More frequent use can increase dryness and irritation. This is a daily acne-treatment routine, not a last-minute face filter.

What if I already use another acne product?

Using another topical acne medication at the same time or immediately afterward makes irritation and dryness more likely. If that happens, use only one at a time. Ask your clinician how PuraClear fits with prescribed treatments.

What if my skin gets dry?

If bothersome dryness or peeling occurs, reduce use to once a day or every other day. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation becomes severe. Moisturizing ingredients don’t make an acne medication irritation-proof.

Know your skincare.

Directions & Drug Facts

Active ingredient / Purpose

Salicylic Acid 0.5% / Acne Treatment

Uses

For the treatment of acne. Penetrates pores to clear most acne blemishes. Helps keep skin clear of new acne pimples, blackheads, or whiteheads.

Warnings

For external use only.

Skin irritation and dryness are more likely if you use another topical acne medication at the same time or immediately following use of this product. If this occurs, use only one topical acne medication at a time.

Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation becomes severe.

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use.

Directions

Clean the skin thoroughly before applying this product. Cover the entire affected area with a thin layer one to three times daily. Because excessive drying of the skin may occur, start with one application daily, then gradually increase to two or three times daily if needed or as directed by a doctor. If bothersome dryness or peeling occurs, reduce application to once a day or every other day.

Other information

Protect the product in this container from excessive heat and direct sun.
